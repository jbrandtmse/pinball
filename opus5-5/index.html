THE JOB

  • Skill shot: soft-plunge into the flashing K-E-Y lane (flippers move it). Full plunge, then make the GETAWAY ramp for a super skill shot.
  • K-E-Y lanes raise the bonus multiplier (up to 6X) and relight the kickback.
  • Knock down the laser grid drop targets to reach the vault. Crack the door, then shoot the vault to lock a ball.
  • Lock 3 balls for VAULT MULTIBALL. Ramps and loops score jackpots; collect all four to light the super jackpot in the vault.
  • 3 loops light START JOB at the hideout. Pick a job with the flippers: Case the Joint, Crack the Safe, Laser Maze, Getaway Drive, Inside Man, Double Cross.
  • Play all six jobs to light THE BIG SCORE, a 4-ball wizard mode worth 100,000,000 in the vault.
  • 12 ramps, 3 completed jobs or your first multiball light an extra ball at the hideout. Hit both yellow standups to light the kickback or spinner.
