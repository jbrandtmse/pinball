RISE OF ATLANTIS

RULE CARD

  • SKILL SHOT — light the flashing lane at launch (50,000)
  • TIDE LANES — complete T-I-D-E for bonus multiplier & kickback
  • TEMPLE COMBO — alternate ramps within 4s, up to 400,000
  • TRIDENTS — light Poseidon's Gift at the whirlpool
  • PEARL BANK — complete to lock balls. 2 locks = KRAKEN MULTIBALL — 3 balls, jackpots 250,000, super jackpot at whirlpool 500,000
  • RISE MODES — hit TEMPEST to start: Tower → Pearls → Tides → Crown
  • POSEIDON'S FURY — complete all modes for the 3-ball wizard: 500,000 jackpots, 1,000,000 super, 5,000,000 CITY AWAKENED
  • EXTRA BALL at 1,500,000 and from Poseidon's Gift
  • BONUS — modes, TIDE completions & Krakens × multiplier (up to 6X)
